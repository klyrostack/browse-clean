BrowseClean Settings

Protection Modules

Configure automated DOM filtering and suppression rules.

Cookie Banners & CMPs Automatically declines or collapses Sourcepoint, OneTrust, Cookiebot, and other consent frameworks.
Newsletter & Promo Overlays Suppresses fullscreen email subscription dialogs and lead capture popups.
Live Chat Widgets Hides persistent customer support and sales chat bubbles (Intercom, Drift, Zendesk, Crisp).
Unblur Soft Paywalls Removes artificial CSS blur filters, max-height cutoffs, and fade overlays on articles.
Anti-Adblock Walls Neutralizes Admiral, BlockAdBlock, and aggressive "disable your adblocker" detection overlays.
Unlock Copy & Right-Click Restores right-click context menus, enables text selection, and blocks clipboard tracking hijackers.
Clean Tracking URLs Strips utm_, fbclid, gclid, and other tracking parameters when copying URLs to clipboard.
Scroll Lock Recovery Restores document body scrolling when a suppressed modal attempts to freeze the page viewport.
Autoplay Video Blocker Prevents background videos from auto-playing sound and video without user gesture.
Survey & Feedback Prompts Suppresses Hotjar, Qualtrics, and on-screen NPS survey prompts.

Domain Whitelist

Domains where BrowseClean is completely inactive. Use this for trusted platforms, internal portals, or sites where popups are required.

Custom CSS Selectors

Target and hide specific persistent layout annoyances that general heuristics do not catch.

Data Management

Backup configuration preferences or restore factory state.

Export Preferences

Import Preferences

Reset to Defaults
